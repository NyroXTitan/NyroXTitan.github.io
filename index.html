About

From schema to screen.

I'm Hammad, a full-stack software engineer and Computer Science graduate of Bahria University, Lahore, most recently an AI Engineer at Horrizon AI.

I like owning a feature completely: designing the data model, building the API, shaping the interface and seeing it through to production. More and more, that also means wiring LLM agents and vision models into products, where they turn raw data into decisions.

I'm just as comfortable heads-down on my own as I am inside a cross-functional team.

How I work

  • Own it end to end

    Data model, API, interface and release. I take responsibility for all of it.

  • Types are contracts

    Shared TypeScript types and clear API contracts keep the front end and back end in sync.

  • Production by default

    Tenant isolation, role-based access and audit trails are built in from the start, not bolted on later.

  • AI with a job to do

    Models and agents earn their place by powering a real feature, not a demo.
